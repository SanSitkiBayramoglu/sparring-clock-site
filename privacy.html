Privacy Policy — Sparring Clock

Last updated: August 19, 2026

The short version

Sparring Clock does not collect, store or share any personal data. Period.

What data we collect

None. The app has no sign-up, no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. It never connects to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your timer settings, custom workouts and training history are stored only locally, on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere and are deleted when you delete the app.

Third parties

We do not use any third-party services, SDKs, advertising networks or analytics providers.

Children's privacy

Since no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
