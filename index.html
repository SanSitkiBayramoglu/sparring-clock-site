FAQ

Does the app need an account or internet?
No. Sparring Clock works fully offline, has no sign-up, no ads and no tracking. Your workouts and history never leave your device.

Why does sound keep playing in the background?
So the round bell and warning cues still reach you while your screen is locked or you are using another app during a workout.

Which languages are supported?
English and Turkish. The app follows your device language.
